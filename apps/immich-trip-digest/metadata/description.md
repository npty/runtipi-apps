# Immich Trips & Digest

**Trip albums.** Every 6 hours it looks at photos that are in no album, groups the ones taken away
from your home places into trips (by Immich's location data), and suggests an album named like
`2026-05 Miyagi–Iwate`. Nothing is created until you press **Approve** on this app's page, where you can
rename the album, add the photos to an existing album from the same dates instead, or dismiss it.
A trip is only suggested once no new away photos have arrived for 2 days.

**Monthly digest.** On the chosen day it sums up the previous month: photos and videos added and their size,
how many are still in no album, new duplicate groups, new unnamed faces, disk space, and whether a newer Immich
release is out.

New suggestions and each digest show up in Immich's notification bell.
With **Dry run** on, the page still works but nothing is written to Immich.
